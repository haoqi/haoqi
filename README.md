# Hi, I'm Haoqi Li 👋

I am a Senior Research Scientist at **Netflix Research & Globalization DSE**, working on **multimodal and multilingual LLMs** for **speech understanding, reasoning, and generation**, with the goal of removing language barriers from the Netflix experience.

Previously, I was a Senior Research Scientist at [Hume AI](https://www.hume.ai/), building the expressive speech generation engine behind its first empathic voice interface, and an Applied Scientist on the [AWS AI](https://aws.amazon.com/ai/) Speech team. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the [Signal Analysis and Interpretation Lab (SAIL)](https://sail.usc.edu/) at USC, advised by [Panayiotis Georgiou](https://www.linkedin.com/in/panayiotisgeorgiou/) and [Shrikanth Narayanan](https://sail.usc.edu/people/shri.html).

🌐 [Website](https://haoqi.github.io/) · 🎓 [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=QqpAM60AAAAJ&hl=en) · 💼 [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/haoqili/)

## 🔭 What I work on

- **Speech generation:** post-training for expressive and controllable TTS and cross-lingual voice
- **Speech recognition and understanding:** multilingual long-form ASR and LLM-based translation
- **Evaluation:** diagnostic audio and multimodal LLM judges informed by human preferences
- **Earlier:** speaker verification, affective computing, and emotion recognition
